## Cross-Domain Foundations

Taste in writing shares the same psychological roots as taste in design and code:

1. **Pattern recognition** -- experienced writers instantly detect filler, hedging, and structural padding
2. **Cognitive load sensitivity** -- good writing reduces the reader's processing effort
3. **Conceptual unity** -- consistent voice and tone across all touchpoints
4. **Audience calibration** -- tone that fits the reader's context and emotional state
5. **Information density** -- every sentence earns its place
6. **Emotional intuition** -- the gut feeling that something "reads wrong" before you can articulate why
