## Cross-Domain Psychological Foundations

Across UX and code, research converges on six pillars of taste:

1. **Pattern recognition from experience** -- experts see structure, not just surface
2. **Sensitivity to cognitive load** -- taste detects and eliminates unnecessary mental effort
3. **Preference for conceptual unity** -- coherent wholes over feature lists
4. **Long-term thinking** -- anticipating change cost, maintenance, evolution
5. **Cultural calibration** -- taste is audience-aware, not universal
6. **Emotional intuition** -- hedonic responses encoded neurally, operating pre-consciously

> "Taste is not just preference. It is trained judgment under uncertainty."
