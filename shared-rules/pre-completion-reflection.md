# Pre-Completion Reflection

Compatibility pointer only. The canonical completion and verification contract
is in `truth-over-comfort.md`, which covers evidence, uncertainty, produced /
verified / delivered state, and worker ownership. Report material side effects
with the result.

Use `principal-liability.md` and `content-policy.md` for risk boundaries,
`capability-discovery.md` before reporting a missing capability, and
`verify-after-fix.md` for operator routing or recurrence checks. Apply those
rules before a completion claim or client-facing closeout; do not emit a
separate reflection checklist or ritual self-check line.
